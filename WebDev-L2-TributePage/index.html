01 WHO HE WAS

A humble man
with a limitless
vision.

Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was a scientist, teacher, author and the 11th President of India. His journey from a small town in Tamil Nadu to the highest office of the nation remains extraordinary.

Born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Kalam came from a modest family. His early life taught him the value of discipline, hard work and education. These values remained central throughout his life.

Through his work with ISRO and DRDO, he became a significant figure in India's space and defence programmes. His contributions to indigenous missile development earned him the title "Missile Man of India."

Beyond science and politics, Kalam's greatest passion was inspiring young minds. He believed that India's future depended on students who were willing to dream, learn and take action.
